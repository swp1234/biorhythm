About Biorhythms: Formula, Cycles & Frequently Asked Questions

Biorhythms calculate periodic sinusoidal waves based on the exact number of elapsed days since birth. The three primary cycles are Physical (23 days), Emotional (28 days), and Intellectual (33 days), modeled by the formula R(t) = sin(2πt / T).

This calculator is an educational self-reflection and wellness tracking tool. It does not provide medical diagnoses or replace clinical chronobiology evaluations.

The Three Primary Cycles

  • Physical Cycle (23 Days): Governs energy levels, physical stamina, coordination, and resistance to fatigue.
  • Emotional Cycle (28 Days): Influences mood swings, nervous system sensitivity, creativity, and empathy.
  • Intellectual Cycle (33 Days): Shapes analytical thinking, mental alertness, memory recall, and logical decision-making.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How are biorhythm curves calculated?

A: Each rhythm starts at birth (day 0) and cycles sinusoidally between +100% (peak vitality) and -100% (restoration phase), crossing zero at half-cycles.

Q: Is biorhythm theory medical science?

A: No. While modern medicine studies circadian rhythms (24h internal clocks governed by the SCN), fixed lifetime biorhythms serve as a fun reflective journaling framework.

Q: What does a 'critical transition day' mean?

A: Critical days occur when a curve crosses the baseline (0%). They signify transitional shifts where mindfulness and pacing yourself are encouraged.

Q: Is my birth date saved or transmitted anywhere?

A: No. All calculations happen entirely in your local browser sandbox. No personal dates are stored or sent to remote servers.
